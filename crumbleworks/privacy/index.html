Crumbleworks

Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026 · Applies to the Crumbleworks game for Android (package com.crumbleworks.game) and its browser version

The short version

  • Crumbleworks has no accounts and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, cash, upgrades, settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run servers that receive it.
  • The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details.

Who we are

Crumbleworks is a demolition puzzle game made by Badlands Interactive ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and who it is shared with. Questions about it can be sent to:

badlandsinteractivestudio@gmail.com

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and preferences in the app's private storage on your phone (or in your browser's storage for the browser version). This includes levels unlocked, stars, best scores, in-game cash, upgrades, trophies, daily-challenge streaks and settings such as sound and vibration. This data stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

The app asks for one Android permission, vibration, used for haptic feedback when buildings fall. You can turn it off in the game's settings.

Advertising

The free Android version of Crumbleworks shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Some ads are optional ("watch an ad to get a reward"); others may appear between levels. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect:

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. It is sent encrypted. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland) the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice later from the game's settings.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on the device).
  • Remove ads: an optional purchase removes ads that appear between levels.

Purchases

Crumbleworks offers optional in-app purchases (for example removing ads, a VIP pack or in-game cash). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play's terms. We receive only what is needed to deliver what you bought: the product, an order ID and a purchase token that confirms the purchase is genuine. We never receive your payment card, bank details or billing address.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera data.
  • We do not run our own analytics or tracking. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

Who data is shared with

The only third parties that receive data from the game are Google as advertising provider (AdMob) and as payment processor (Google Play), for the purposes above. Data may also be disclosed if the law requires it.

Children

Crumbleworks is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent and believe your child's information has been collected through the game's ads, contact us and we will help you have it removed with Google.

Keeping and deleting data

Game progress is kept on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its storage (Settings › Apps › Crumbleworks › Storage › Clear data). We hold no copy. Data collected by Google for ads is kept according to Google's own retention policies; you can manage it at My Ad Center and by resetting your advertising ID.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA or California's CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or limit its use, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal information about you, most requests are best handled through the controls above or directly with Google. You can always write to us and we will help.

Security

Game data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Data sent by the ads SDK and Google Play travels over encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts using information in a new way (for example cloud saves or analytics), we will update this page and change the effective date at the top before the new version of the game is released.
